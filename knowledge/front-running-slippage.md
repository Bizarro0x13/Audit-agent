# Front-running and missing slippage protection
Pending transactions are public. Someone can insert a transaction before or after yours to profit from your trade or action. Swaps and deposits with no minimum output or deadline can be sandwiched.
Look for: swaps with a zero minimum out, approvals or claims that can be raced, first-come rewards, commit steps that reveal the answer.
Common fixes: slippage limits and deadlines, commit-reveal, private transaction submission.
